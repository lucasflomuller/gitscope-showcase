## Dependencies installed by the user

The source distribution contains no `node_modules`, bundled runtime, generated
`.next` output, third-party WASM grammar files, font files or native binaries.
Dependencies are referenced by `package.json` and `bun.lock` and retain the notices
in their distributions. `docs/THIRD-PARTY.md` records the reviewed installed metadata.
A binary/container distribution would require a separate review of the included
packages and their notice/source obligations, including native libvips.
